What an ambassador may do

  • Welcome, answer and praise. The whole point of the role, and the desk makes each of them one press.
  • Silence a nation in a chat, for up to 24 hours. That is the only power here that takes anything away.
  • Pass everything else up. Deleting a line, hiding a nation, lifting a silence and reading an account are a moderator's, and stay a moderator's.
  • You see nations, never the people behind them: no email, no sign-in, no payment. Every press is written to the audit log under your name.

The limits, in plain words

  • Up to 24 hours, and you must pick a reason from the list. The nation is told an ambassador did it.
  • You cannot silence another ambassador, a moderator, or a nation in your own alliance.
  • You cannot lift a silence, your own included. If you got it wrong, pass it to a moderator and say so.
  • Every one of these sits in the admin audit log with your name, the line and the reason, and an admin reads them.
