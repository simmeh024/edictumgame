The referee’s die

The Intelligence card already rolls a die in front of the player and marks the row it landed on. An attack costs far more and shows nothing but a line of text, although the referee returns the roll. The four panels are the same attack, one state each: the forecast, the roll, a win and a loss. The attack factors move out of the paragraph and into a list, so the forecast and the report read the same way.
