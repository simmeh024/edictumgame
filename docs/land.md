# Land deals

Asked by the owner on 21 November 2026: "add a land trading system", ideas first. Of 22 ideas the owner picked 1 (hex sales), 3 (open and uneven swaps), 4 (whole provinces), 6 (gifts), 7 (payment set aside), 8 (instalments), 9 (land valuer), 10 (stamp duty), 11 (land market), 12 (for-sale signs on the map), 14 (auctions), 16 (the map's rules), 19 (ransoms) and 22 (news, history, honours and land prices).

- **Server**: `functions/land.js` (civic ops `ldOffer`, `ldAnswer`, `ldWithdraw`, `ldBid`, `ldClose`, `ldCede`, `ldFail`, `ldPay`, `ldCall`; `ldDrop` on account deletion) and `functions/land-rules.js` (`LD_RULES`, the shape of a deal, bids, instalments, stamp duty). Data `land/{uid}_{x}`, readable by everyone (firestore.rules), written only by the server. The server keeps no land and no stock.
- **Game**: the block "land deals" before "joint ventures" in `game/edictum.html`. `LD_RULES` must match the server's (`test/land.test.mjs`).
  - **Offering**: select a hex of yours on the main map and choose **Trade this land**, or another player's hex and **Ask to buy or swap**. In the editor, select more hexes (yours to give, theirs to receive), choose who it is with (or **Anyone: list it on the land market**, or **Anyone: put it up for auction**), and say what each side pays. **Add the whole province** takes every hex of one of your provinces that can change hands.
  - **Payment set aside**: a payer's game takes its payment from its stock into its own record (`n.lde [{id, r, t}]`) and saves before it offers, accepts, buys or bids; the server reads it there. A bid raised sets aside only the difference. Payments go back when a deal is declined, withdrawn, lapses, is called off, or a bid is beaten.
  - **Handing over**: once agreed, each game hands over its own hexes (`n.bx`, as border swaps do: only a hex's holder can give it away) and tells the server (`ldCede`), which reads them in the saved record. Done once every side that gives land has. Not handed over within 3 days, either side's game calls it off; a game that no longer holds a hex it gives calls it off at once. Land already handed over in a deal that is called off is handed back by the game that received it.
  - **Payment**: once done, each payee's game takes in what was set aside for it, less the **stamp duty** (a tenth), once (`n.ldr`); the payer's game lets its entry go.
  - **Instalments** (7, 14 or 30 days, from the side that receives land): the payer's game sets one aside a day and the server counts it (`ldPay`). More than 2 late, the payee's game calls the deal in (`ldCall`), and the payer's game hands the land back; the payer can also stop paying. What was paid stays paid.
  - **Auctions** run 24 or 48 hours, bids in one resource from a reserve, each beating the last by 5% (at least 1); a bid in the last ten minutes keeps it open ten minutes more. The seller's or a bidder's game closes it (`ldClose`): the highest bid wins.
  - **The map's rules**: land must touch the side that receives it (each hex next to its land or to another hex of the deal); no capital, no hex with one of the giver's cities, no region wonder's site (wonder, lighthouse, dam); nothing that cuts the giver's land off from its capital. Nations at war deal only in **ransoms** (land taken in war going back to its nation, cities included). A deal drawn on another map version holds nothing.
  - **Land valuer**: a hex's worth from its yields (food, timber, iron, oil), an iron deposit, a river, a coast and a site, in stock at today's market prices (`economy().price`), scaled by the median of what the region's last 30 days of sales paid against it once there are three.
  - **Where it shows**: Diplomacy › **Land** tab (offers, deals being handed over and paid, your offers, the land market, recent deals); the map's hex panel; gold hatching and a pennant on land for sale or at auction (Map layers › Land for sale); the bell; the region's happenings; the nation's history (`log` kind `land`); Statistics › Economy › **Land prices**; Help › Land deals.
  - Eleven achievements, `dip-ld*`. Land offers count as trade offers in Settings › Privacy › Who can reach me.
- **Limits**: 8 deals open at once per nation (offers, listings, auctions, deals being handed over), 2 auctions, 40 hexes a side, 5,000 of a resource in a payment, 500 a day in instalments. An offer waits 7 days, a listing 14.
- **Tests**: `test/land.test.mjs` (in `test:fx`) and `test/land-e2e.mjs` (`npm run test:land`, CI suite `land`).
