Privacy notice

Last updated 26 October 2026

In short. Edictum keeps what it needs to run the game: your player ID, your nation, your posts and, if you link one, your Google sign-in. Your nation and posts are public. There are no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. You can delete everything yourself at any time from the account menu.

Who is responsible

Edictum (edictumgame.com) is a free online game run by Pascal Slettenaar, a private individual in Mexico, who is responsible for your personal data (the responsable under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties, the LFPDPPP). Write to p.slettenaar@gmail.com with any question or request.

What the game keeps

  • Your account. When you open the game you get a guest account straight away: a random player ID, kept by Firebase Authentication. If you link a Google account, it also keeps the email address, name and profile picture address that Google shares, and your Google account ID, so that you can sign in on any device.
  • Your nation. Everything you create in the game: your nation's name, flag, motto and description, its statistics, land, cities, laws and history, your messages to other nations, and when you last played.
  • Your posts on the region message boards, with the time and your player ID.
  • Reports. If you report another player, the report keeps your player ID, what you reported and why, and any note you add. Only the moderators see it.
  • Moderation. If a moderator hides or removes your nation, or bans your account, the game keeps that decision and the reason.
  • Technical data. The servers that deliver the game see your IP address and browser details when you load the page or sign in, and keep them in logs for a short time for security and to stop abuse.
  • Storage in your browser. The game keeps your nation, your settings and your sign-in in your browser's local storage, session storage and IndexedDB. The game needs these to work. It uses no cookies, web beacons or other technologies to track you or to show adverts.

The game asks for no sensitive personal data (such as health, beliefs or origin). Please do not put any personal information in your nation's texts or your posts: they are public.

What it is used for

Only to run the game for you, and to keep it safe and fair (reports, moderation and logs). These purposes are all needed for the game to work; there are no secondary purposes such as marketing, advertising or profiling, and the data is never sold. For players in the European Union, the legal bases are the agreement in the terms (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and the game's legitimate interest in keeping it safe (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

What other players see

Your nation (its name, flag, motto, description, statistics, land and history) and your board posts are public: every player, and anyone who looks at the game's data, can see them. Your email address, your Google account and your reports are not public.

Who processes it, and transfers

The game runs on Google Firebase (Hosting, Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which Google provides as a processor (encargado) under its data processing terms; Google only processes the data to run the game. The game's database is in the European Union (Cloud Firestore, the eur3 locations in Belgium and the Netherlands), and Firebase Authentication and Google's delivery network may process data in other countries, including the United States. Your data is not shared with anyone else, unless a competent authority requires it by law. When you link a Google account, Google shares your details with the game under Google's privacy policy. The game's fonts are served from edictumgame.com itself, so no other company sees your visit.

How long it is kept

  • Your account, nation and posts: until you delete them. A nation that has not been played for 250 days becomes dormant and disappears from other players' view, but is kept so that you can wake it.
  • Reports: until a moderator has dealt with them, then they are deleted.
  • Backups: the database is backed up daily (kept 14 days) and can be restored to any moment of the last 7 days. Anything you delete is gone from the backups within 14 days.

Deleting everything, and withdrawing consent

Open the account menu (at the foot of the side bar, or at the top of the founding page) and choose Delete my nation and account. That deletes your nation, every post you wrote and your account at once, and is the simplest way to withdraw your consent. If you play as a guest and your guest sign-in is too old to be removed, an empty guest ID without any data may remain in Firebase Authentication.

Your rights (ARCO and more)

You may access the data the game keeps about you, have it rectified, have it cancelled (deleted), and oppose its use; players in the European Union may also restrict its use and receive it in a portable form. Most of this you can do in the game. Otherwise, write to p.slettenaar@gmail.com with your name, your player ID (shown in the account menu), the right you want to exercise and what it concerns. You will get an answer within 20 business days, and a decision in your favour is carried out within 15 business days after that.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the data protection authority: in Mexico, the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno; in the European Union, the authority of the country where you live.

Children

Edictum is not meant for children. If you are under 18, play only with your parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date at the top.
